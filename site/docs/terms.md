## Who can have an account

- You must be **13 or older**, or sign in with a **school account your school approved** for Zenith.
  Zenith does not ask your age; by making an account you confirm that this is true for you. Anyone can
  use Zenith without an account.
- Anyone who may have an account can make a team and own it. A team does not need an adult, but we suggest that a
  coach or mentor holds the admin role.
- Give a display name your teammates will recognise. Do not use anyone else's name, and do not put
  personal details (an address, a phone number, a school) in a display name, team name or auto title.

## Your autos belong to your team

- **Your team owns its autos** and every file it publishes to its library. We claim no ownership.
- To run Zenith, you give Horizon permission to **store your team's files, copy them as needed to keep
  them safe and working, and show them only to the people your team chooses**: your team's members, and
  the teams or link holders you share with. We use this permission only to run Zenith. It ends when the
  files are deleted (see the [retention policy](retention.md)).
- When you share outside your team, the other team gets a summary of your auto, not the file itself:
  where your robot goes and when, its outer size, and the field elements it takes. Anyone who watches
  your robot in a match can see much the same.
- Only publish autos you have the right to publish. If another team shared a path with you, do not
  republish it as your own without asking them.

## Acceptable use

Be the kind of teammate FIRST's Gracious Professionalism asks for. Do not use Zenith to:

- harass, bully or threaten anyone, or post anything hateful, sexual or violent in a name, a title,
  a comment or a proposal;
- collect other people's personal information;
- pretend to be another person or another team, or claim a team number that is not yours;
- try to read what you were not given, get around the database's access rules, or break or overload
  the service (for example, opening a link over and over to use up its free allowance);
- upload anything harmful, such as malware, or anything that is not yours to share;
- break the law or FIRST's rules.

If you find a way into something you should not see, stop and tell us privately (see
[Contact](#contact)). Please do not test it on other teams' data.

## Team numbers

- One team in Zenith holds each FTC team number. A number is marked **unverified** until a Horizon
  admin checks it. Unverified teams can use every feature.
- To be verified, a team asks for a review and gives evidence, such as a photo of the robot or banner
  with a code, or an event result that lists the team. A Horizon admin reviews it and records a
  one-sentence reason your team can read.
- If a team claims a number another team already holds and has not been verified, both are marked
  **contested** and a Horizon admin reviews the claims. Both teams keep working meanwhile. A claim on a
  verified number is refused; ask us for a review if the holder is wrong.
- The team that does not get the number keeps its library and members, under its name without a
  number. It can claim another number.
- The team that does not get the number may **appeal once, within 14 days** of the decision. A
  Horizon admin decides the appeal, possibly the same one who made the first decision, and that
  decision is final.
- Every decision records which Horizon admin made it, and both teams can read that in the number's
  history. A Horizon admin may decide about a team they are a member of, Horizon's own included; the
  record shows it.

A team's owner or admin requests verification in the app, with **Request verification** on the
team's page, and the request waits in a review queue that only Horizon admins can see.

## Ending your account

- **You can stop at any time.** Delete your account in the app's settings, or leave a team, or delete a
  team you own. Deleting a team deletes its members, invites, settings, activity and library for
  everyone in it, so download anything you want to keep first; the
  [retention policy](retention.md#when-you-delete-a-team) says what is kept.
- **We may remove content or suspend an account or team** that breaks these terms, or when the law
  requires it. Where we can, we will tell the team why.
- **If we ever shut down the cloud features,** we will say so in the app and on this site well in
  advance, so that teams can download their autos first.
